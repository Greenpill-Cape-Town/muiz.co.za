title: "Marine Estate Connected Communities"
description: "Connecting the Marine Estate community in Muizenberg — one group at a time."
---
The *CCME* (Connected Community Marine Estate) is a growing Muizenberg based network of around 230 residents connected through 13 interconnected WhatsApp circles, each focused on strengthening relationships and improving life in our neighbourhood of Marine Estate. At the heart of the community is the General Community Circle, our common meeting place where newcomers begin, community-wide decisions are made, and collaboration across all circles is nurtured. Around it are special-interest circles including Greening & Vlei Care, Repair Café, Recycling & Waste Collective, Play at Zandvlei, Film Circle, Tech4Good, The Dignity Project in Vryground, MLRA Coordination Circle, the Ventnor & Windermere Verge Garden group and a Leaders Support Circle. Together these circles enable residents to contribute where their interests, skills, and passions are strongest while remaining connected to the wider community.

Our shared purpose is simple: *to build a more connected community* in Marine Estate. We are guided by the principles of Sociocracy, using consent-based decision-making to encourage participation and shared ownership, and Nonviolent Communication (NVC) to cultivate empathy, understanding, and constructive dialogue. We also draw inspiration from approaches such as design thinking, asset-based community development, Open Space Technology, and appreciative inquiry, fostering practical collaboration, local leadership, and a neighbourhood where people know, support, and create positive change together.
