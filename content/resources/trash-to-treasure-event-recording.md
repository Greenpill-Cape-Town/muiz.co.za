**Trash to Treasure** brought together artists, musicians, community organisers and curious visitors at The Commons on 5 June 2026 for a lively celebration of creativity, reuse and environmental imagination.

The [event recording](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13n_2PCGBXFeA-ToyYhLNreynfZsThfVw/view?usp=sharing), the [looping film](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Os1p0V5R-5doCffyviGrxr741Q7HnNM0/view?usp=sharing) during the event with introductions, and [the animation "Message in a Bottle"](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wfKRScIuieVfxyVebaI-kiHm04iLg9pc/view?usp=sharing) are all publicly available - we hope you enjoy it with us!


Created as a community art initiative connecting artists and artmakers from Muizenberg and Vrygrond, the physical and online exhibition featured work by Angela McPherson, Harry Charalambos, Jupiter Thompson, Heleen Verwey, Tova Luck, Tyler Pike, Nicola Schorn, Ruby D’Almar, Lizzie Kruger and Dale Lawrence.

Each artist brought a distinct perspective to the theme, showing how discarded materials, overlooked objects and environmental concerns can be transformed into art that is playful, thoughtful and unexpected.

The evening extended well beyond the gallery walls. Acclaimed musician **Pedro Espri-Sanchez** took attendees on a participatory musical journey, demonstrating how waste materials can be transformed into instruments, rhythm and shared experience.

Guests also attended the world premiere and first public showing of **Message in a Bottle**, an animated film conceived by the Berg Waste and Recycling Collective and created by Chloé van Wyk.

The exhibition included a digital installation of the **Lattice** project, adding an interactive dimension to the evening, while the new **Berg Waste and Recycling Collective website** was revealed publicly for the first time (this website!).

Held in celebration of World Environment Day, *Trash to Treasure* was filled with colour, music, animation, conversation and fresh ideas. It invited everyone present to look again at what we throw away—and imagine what it might become.

The evening was generously sponsored by the Greenpill Cape Town Chapter.
